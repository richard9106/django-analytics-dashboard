Therapy practice management software for calmer private practice operations.

A therapy practice management platform should help clinicians protect time, reduce administrative drift, and keep sensitive client workflows organized without feeling cold or complicated.

What therapists need in one workspace

NuviaMy brings together client records, appointment scheduling, clinical notes, treatment plans, billing, documents, intake packets, portal access, reminders, and Google integrations. This matters because therapy practices rarely struggle with one isolated task; they struggle with small handoffs between many sensitive tasks.

Key workflows to evaluate

  • Practice-scoped clients, clinicians, appointments, notes, invoices, and documents.
  • Scheduling with recurring sessions, availability settings, and calendar sync.
  • Clinical documentation for progress notes, intake notes, diagnoses, goals, objectives, and treatment plan reviews.
  • Billing workflows for invoices, superbills, packages, insurance payer settings, and follow-up.
  • A client portal that separates client access from internal clinical operations.

Built around privacy-aware defaults

Therapy software should make secure behavior easier. NuviaMy is designed around tenant isolation, client-only portal access, role-aware redirects, and focused server-rendered workflows so practices can review compliance needs with a clear operational foundation.
