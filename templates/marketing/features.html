Core Features

Feature areas built around real therapy operations.

Scheduling and calendar

Day, week, month, and year calendar views, availability rules, recurring appointments, conflict validation, drag-and-drop rescheduling, and Google Calendar sync.

Public booking requests

Share a public booking link, collect appointment requests, review them inside Requests, then approve to create the client and appointment.

Clinical notes

Progress notes, intake notes, general notes, diagnoses, treatment plan links, review workflows, and note locking for a stronger documentation routine.

Treatment plans

Build treatment plans with goals, objectives, interventions, target dates, statuses, and review dates that surface when follow-up is due.

Billing and superbills

Invoices, printable invoices, superbills, service packages, usage tracking, insurance payer settings, and billing follow-up tasks.

Client portal

Client-only access for appointments, visible documents, invoices, intake packets, service packages, and appointment change requests.

Documents

Upload documents, control client visibility, download/delete files, prepare for R2 production storage, and export selected files to Google Drive.

Reminders and Gmail

Gmail-powered appointment reminders, reminder statuses, error tracking, and scheduled reminder delivery for upcoming sessions.

Google workspace

OAuth-based Google connection for Gmail, Calendar, and Drive without storing Google passwords, with encrypted tokens at rest.

Security-minded

Practice-scoped by default.

Every sensitive object belongs to a practice. Client users stay inside the portal, internal workflows redirect client roles away, and Google integrations use OAuth instead of stored passwords.
