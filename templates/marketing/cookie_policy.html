Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. NuviaMy uses only the cookies needed to operate the website and authenticated workspace.

Cookies we use

  • Session cookies: keep you signed in and associate requests with your secure Django session.
  • CSRF cookies: protect forms from cross-site request forgery.
  • Tour preference: with your permission, the dashboard guided tour uses browser local storage to remember when it has been completed.

What we do not use

NuviaMy does not currently use advertising cookies, behavioral tracking pixels, or third-party analytics cookies on the public website.

Third-party services

When you choose an integration or payment flow, Google and Stripe may set or read cookies on their hosted pages. Their use is governed by their own policies. NuviaMy does not use those cookies for advertising.

Your choices

Choose Accept preferences to remember completion of the guided tour, or Essential only to use the site without that optional storage. Essential only also removes any previous saved tour preference. Your choice is stored separately for 180 days in nuviamy.cookieChoices so we can respect it. You can change your choice using Cookie settings in the footer. Session and CSRF cookies remain necessary for sign-in and secure forms. You can also clear or block storage in your browser.

Contact

For questions about cookies or privacy, use the NuviaMy support form.
