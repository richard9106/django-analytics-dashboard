Questions before you start

Do I have to connect Google?

No. You can create clients, set availability and manage appointments without Google. Connect Calendar or Gmail only if you want those optional features.

What is sent to Google Calendar?

The current sync sends an occupancy block with a generic title and the appointment’s start and end times. Open NuviaMy for the client details. Review the Google data disclosures before connecting.
