Client portal guide

Client portal software for therapists that keeps communication organized.

A therapy client portal should make common client tasks easier without opening internal clinical workflows to the wrong audience.

What clients can handle in a focused portal

NuviaMy gives clients a dedicated portal experience for upcoming appointments, shared documents, visible invoices, intake packets, service package context, and appointment change requests.

Why portal boundaries matter

  • Client users are redirected away from internal practice pages.
  • Portal access links each login to one client record and one practice.
  • Practices decide which documents are visible to the client.
  • Requests flow into an inbox so therapists can review before changing schedules or records.

Better intake and follow-up

Therapy intake forms, appointment requests, shared files, and client questions become easier to track when they live near scheduling, billing, and documentation instead of scattered across email threads.

Create a calmer client experience.

Use NuviaMy to connect intake, appointment visibility, documents, invoices, and portal requests in one practice-scoped workspace.
