Pricing FAQ

Questions therapists ask before choosing practice management software.

Can I pay monthly?

Yes. Monthly subscriptions are available for solo therapists, group practices, and clinics.

Do yearly plans include a discount?

Yes. Yearly billing includes a 10% discount compared with paying monthly for 12 months.

Is this for solo therapists or clinics?

NuviaMy supports solo private practices, group therapy practices, counselors, psychologists, behavioral health providers, and mental health clinics.

Is NuviaMy HIPAA certified?

NuviaMy is HIPAA-aware and built around tenant isolation and secure defaults, but practices should complete their own compliance review before using it for production clinical operations.
