Documentation as part of operations

Notes are most useful when they connect with scheduling, billing, documents, and portal workflows. NuviaMy keeps those connections close while maintaining a calm interface for sensitive work.

Example: from a session to a reviewed note

Open the client’s appointment, create the appropriate note and connect relevant treatment-plan context. Review the content before locking the record. Keep clinical details inside the practice workflow rather than in calendar descriptions or general support messages.

Keep the plan connected to the work

A treatment plan can track goals, objectives, interventions and review dates. Use those review dates to return to the plan as care progresses, rather than treating the plan as an isolated document.

Questions before you start

Are my clinical notes automatically shared with clients?

The client portal is separate from the internal workspace. Share only the documents and information you intend the client to see, and review visibility before sharing.

Does the software replace my recordkeeping obligations?

No. Your practice remains responsible for the accuracy, review, retention and lawful use of its clinical records. Review the service agreements and applicable requirements for your practice.
