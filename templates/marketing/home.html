Security-Minded Foundation

Designed with healthcare privacy patterns from the start.

Tenant isolation

Every sensitive object belongs to a practice, and internal workflows are scoped so teams only see their own clients, notes, appointments, invoices, documents, and plans.

Client-only portal access

Client users are restricted to the portal, temporary passwords require change, and sensitive workflows are protected from accidental internal access.
