Before you connect Google

This optional connection identifies your Google account by email and stores encrypted access tokens to run the capabilities you select. Authorized members of your practice can use the connection; it is shared with your practice.

Selected Gmail access sends recipients, subjects and message contents, or reads recent message headers and previews for your inbox display. Calendar access reads upcoming event titles and times and synchronizes generic appointment occupancy blocks. Drive access exports selected document names and contents and manages the configured folder. Google and our hosting provider process the information needed to provide these features; email recipients receive sent messages.

We follow Google's Limited Use requirements. We do not sell Google data, use it for advertising or use it to train generalized AI models. Disconnecting clears active credentials and stops the connection; it does not revoke Google's authorization or delete sent emails, external events/files or retained backups.

Read Google data use, protection, retention and deletion details (opens a new tab). You can also remove NuviaMy access in your Google Account (opens a new tab).

Choose your capabilities above, then select Connect Google to continue to Google's authorization screen. You can decline authorization there.
